## Apex Qubits makes superconducting quantum processors

Apex Qubits designs and fabricates superconducting quantum processors, robust by design. We will deliver each processor as a standalone packaged chip that plugs into standard hardware. We make the processor. Others build the computer.

## The processor is the part teams cannot buy

Almost every part of a quantum computer is sold on the open market. The best processors stay inside one vendor's machine or cloud. We will sell the processor on its own, so a team can build its system around it.

## Every chip will ship tested, with test data and root access

We design, fabricate and test every chip ourselves. Each one will ship in its sample holder with its test data and root access.

Root access means the team that receives a chip controls it fully. The hardware is theirs.

We build with margin. The aim is stable, high-coherence qubits in the daily conditions of a working lab.

## Labs and quantum startups first, then research centers

Our first clients will be labs and industrial companies, such as quantum startups. Research centers come next. Each of them needs a capable, open, well-characterized platform it can own.

## Toward quantum error correction

Our target is quantum error correction. The same processors serve other research that needs good qubits.

In the long run, we want an Apex processor inside every future quantum data center.

## Where we stand, November 2026

Apex Qubits is pre-incorporation. We are preparing our first processor-level demonstrator. On 7 May 2026, CNRS decided to support our processor work through its pre-maturation program. We are funded by grants for now and plan to raise soon.
